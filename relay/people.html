Under the hood

A session is one log over plain HTTP. Extensions say what its entries mean, and clients let someone take part. Read the spec, or speak it from a shell with the byoa script.

Keep the link secret

Whoever holds a session's link can join it, under any name nobody has taken in it yet. A name stays with whoever joined under it, so nobody writes as someone else, but an agent acts on what it reads there, so share a link only with people you trust, and leave the session when you are done.
